A control from finding to follow-up

Follow the evidence for one failed check.

  1. The control is marked Not met, with the scan result attached.
  2. The reviewer opens the evidence and assigns a fix to the appropriate owner.
  3. After the change, the team reruns the scan and reviews the new result before closing the item.

What you can do in PCS

Risk assessment

PCS marks every safeguard that applies to your company as met, not met, or lacking evidence, and explains in one line why any safeguard falls short.

Regulatory compliance

PCS presents each regulation requirement by requirement. A requirement counts as met only when every control behind it is met.

Risk register

Every safeguard you haven’t met is added to the risk register automatically. It remains there until someone fixes it or documents why the company accepts the risk.

Scans

Skopein scans your systems on a schedule, and PCS attaches each result to the control it supports.

Remediation

For every failed security check, PCS provides easy-to-follow manual fixes, and remediation scripts for bulk updates. Once the fix is applied, the next scan verifies its effectiveness and PCS updates the control’s compliance status accordingly.

Policies and plans

PCS provides eighteen security policies that are ready to adopt, along with an incident response plan and a disaster recovery plan.

Filings and attestations

PCS stores your compliance filing receipts and the signed attestations that serve as evidence where a scan can’t reach.

Assign training and record completions.

The training library contains 560 narrated courses, 14 documentaries, and 4 games. PCS records each completion against the employee and files it as evidence that your staff has been trained.
