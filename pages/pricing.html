AI in PCS

What AI does for you inside PCS.

  • Vulnerability watch. Checks new vulnerabilities daily against your actual equipment and software, and sends plain-English fix steps only for what you own.
  • Connectors on request. Builds the integration your business needs, beyond Microsoft and Google.
  • Cyber insurance applications. Pre-fills carrier renewal questionnaires from the evidence already in PCS.
  • Security questionnaires. Answers SIG and client due-diligence questionnaires from the evidence on file.
  • Vendor SOC 2 review. Reads a vendor’s SOC 2 report and pulls out the exceptions, the controls you must handle on your side, and the expiry date.
  • Regulation watch. Flags changes to NY DFS, HIPAA and CMMC, and shows which of your controls they affect.
  • Framework mapping. Maps every result to each framework that applies to you.
  • Reports written for your business. Analyzes your scan data and writes two reports specific to your business: one for management, covering risk and priorities in plain English, and one for your IT support, covering the exact settings to fix and how.
  • Business system review. Works through the systems that run your business, such as agency management, practice management, CRM and billing systems, the way an administrator would, reading their security settings and checking each one against the required security parameters.
  • Policy tailoring. Fills Pistos templates with your real facts, so policies match how you actually work.
  • Incident help. Walks your staff through the playbook, builds the timeline, and drafts the 72-hour DFS notice.
  • Annual certification package. Assembles the evidence for your DFS filing and flags gaps before the deadline.
  • Auditor assistant. Answers auditor questions with direct links to the evidence.
  • In-app help. Answers “how do I” questions for your administrators inside PCS.

Our guardrails

  • AI drafts, Pistos approves. Nothing changes a compliance status without human review.
  • Connectors are read-only and use their own logged credentials.
  • Your data is never used to train AI models.
  • Every AI action is logged and visible to your auditor.
