Every scan, including the one-time assessment, comes with:

Management report

Each test area in plain English: what we found, what it means for the business, and how urgent it is.

Technical report

Every finding with the setting behind it, the evidence appendix, and each result scored against the control it answers.

Step-by-step remediation tasks

A task list for every vulnerability noted, in priority order, that your IT person or provider can work straight through.

Readout call

A readout call comes with each scan cycle, quarterly on Skopein Multi-site.

  • Every plan scans Windows, macOS and Linux computers, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, email authentication, sign-in logs, and supported business applications (HawkSoft, then AMS360) as their scans are released
  • Additional endpoints cost $4 per endpoint per month in every plan
  • An extra scan on demand, after an incident or before a third-party review, costs $750 on Skopein Cloud and Skopein Network
  • The web application scan add-on costs $750 per application per scan
  • Onboarding is the same as PCS and carries over, so you can move to PCS later with no second onboarding fee
  • The one-time assessment fee is credited in full toward PCS or a Skopein plan within 90 days
  • Microsoft 365 Business Premium is recommended, not required
  • Managed service providers and vCISOs receive a 35% wholesale discount off list

Skopein plans cover the scans and the reports. The risk register, policies, disaster recovery and incident response plans, training, auditor logins and the filing package are part of PCS, and every PCS plan already includes Skopein.
