The founder

Pete Sfoglia, Founder

Pete founded Pistos after more than 26 years advising large institutions on cybersecurity compliance as a partner at Ernst & Young, Accenture, PricewaterhouseCoopers, and Wipro. His clients included some of the largest financial institutions and energy companies in the United States. He mentored George Kurtz, the CEO of CrowdStrike, and Jon Darbyshire, the founder of Archer Technologies and SmartSuite.

“The compliance problems facing organizations under 500 employees are identical to the ones I solved for Fortune 100 institutions. The difference is that those institutions had a hundred-person security function. Pistos is what I would have built for them if they had to do it alone.”
Pete Sfoglia, Founder
