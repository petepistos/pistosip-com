From vulnerability to remediation

  1. Scan reports explain what the findings mean for management and show IT the settings behind them.
  2. Review the recommended fix for each reported gap. Where a script is available, your IT team should review and test it before use.
  3. Rescan to check whether the finding is resolved.
  4. PCS stores dated scan evidence so you can compare before and after.
