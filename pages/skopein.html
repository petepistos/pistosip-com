External scanner

From vulnerability to remediation

  1. Every scan produces a plain-language management report and a technical report for your IT support.
  2. Every failed check comes with step-by-step instructions and a remediation script. You decide which vulnerabilities to fix.
  3. The next scan confirms that the fix worked.
  4. AI maintains the scanners and keeps their checks current.
