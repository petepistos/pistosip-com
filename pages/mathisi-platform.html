Mathisi · Μάθηση · Security Awareness

Training your staff will actually finish. Records your examiner will actually accept.

Mathisi is the Pistos security awareness platform: 45 narrated, interactive tutorials across three depth levels, knowledge checks on every lesson, and phishing simulation with after-click coaching. It runs inside the same platform as your compliance program, so every completion becomes dated evidence, not a certificate lost in someone's inbox.

◆

Curriculum

Three levels, one path

Every employee starts with the eleven required beginner topics: the vocabulary, the everyday threats, and the habits that prevent most incidents. Staff with elevated access continue into twenty-three intermediate topics tuned to their workflows, and IT leadership trains at the advanced level. Each lesson is professionally narrated, with a different voice for each level, so a course feels like a briefing rather than a slideshow.

Interactive Lessons

Explore, listen, then prove it

Each topic is built around an annotated visual guide: staff click through the numbered sections, listen to the narration, and finish with a knowledge check on that exact material. Nobody passes by letting a video play in another tab. The format rewards attention, and the quiz results show who actually absorbed it.

Phishing Simulation

Practice against the real thing

Realistic phishing campaigns built on the lures agencies actually receive, delivered to your staff on a schedule you approve. Every click is tracked, and anyone who engages lands on a coaching page instead of a lecture. No shame, no spectacle, just repetition until the reflex changes.

Evidence

Training that counts as compliance

Mathisi lives inside Pistos Compliance Sentinel, so training sits alongside your risk assessment, policies, and IR/DR plan. Required beginner modules are tracked to completion with a visible progress bar, intermediate and advanced tracks are there for staff who need them, and when an examiner or underwriter asks for workforce training documentation, the dated record is already in the platform.

Materiality First

Training the people who handle the data that matters most.

Generic awareness training spends the same effort on everyone. Mathisi puts the deepest training where the risk is: the staff who touch client records, move money, and hold privileged access. The people closest to regulated data get trained like it, because that is where a single mistake carries the real cost.
