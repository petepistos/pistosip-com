Why now: the attacker has a new assistant

So, why now? Why are third parties so worried about their data that resides on your systems?

The emergence of AI has exposed small and mid-sized businesses to a growing wave of cyberattacks. AI has made skilled attackers more capable, but its greater impact is putting sophisticated tools into the hands of people with little technical expertise. The result is a dramatic increase in potential attackers, with the cost of launching an attack approaching zero.

Customers and regulators have responded with stricter security requirements and demands for documented evidence.

For your business, the question is no longer whether cybersecurity matters, but whether you can demonstrate that your business is adequately protected.
